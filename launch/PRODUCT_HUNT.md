## 💬 First Comment / Maker Story (To post immediately upon launch)

> Hey Product Hunt! 👋
> 
> I'm Lithish, creator of **Gronckle**.
>
> Like most developers, I spend hours doom-scrolling GitHub, Hacker News, and Twitter looking for the right libraries. But modern developer discovery has a problem: hype and marketing often overshadow genuinely incredible, lightweight, high-performance open-source tools.
>
> So I built **Gronckle** — themed around an all-knowing dragon who sleeps on a hoard of the best developer repositories and tools in existence.
>
> ### 🐉 What makes Gronckle different:
> 1. **The Smelt (AI Stack Architect):** Tell Gronckle what you want to build in plain English. Our LLM edge functions analyze curated tools, match real dependencies, and export a ready-to-run `package.json` or formatted `README.md`.
> 2. **Vector Similarity (pgvector):** Real semantic cosine similarity to discover "Similar Stacks" you didn't even know existed.
> 3. **The Stash:** Live GitHub stars and metadata via batched Redis-cached edge functions — zero rate limits.
> 4. **Community Submissions:** Anyone can submit an underrated repository, and the community upvotes the best finds.
> 5. **Pure Developer UX:** `⌘K` global search palette, retro interactive hacker terminal in The Cave, and warm `#FF6B2B` ember glow on a pure black background.
>
> The entire project is **100% open-source** on GitHub!
>
> I'd love your feedback, feature ideas, or submissions of your favorite underrated developer tools!
>
> Happy building (and happy resting),  
> Lithish 🐲🔥
